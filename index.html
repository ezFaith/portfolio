About

Most people pick a side. I work on both. I'm the person who keeps a live business online: the ERP, the POS terminals, the network and every endpoint that touches them. When something breaks, I fix it. When something looks off, I want to know why. That second instinct pulled me into security, where I build tools that spot attacks hiding in network traffic and turn thousands of noisy logs, flows and alerts into something a person can act on.
